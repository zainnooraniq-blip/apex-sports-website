Our Services

Esports

We've moved into competitive gaming. From sponsoring tournaments to creating events from the ground up — Apex now operates across the esports ecosystem.

A New Frontier

Sport, Without Borders

Esports is one of the fastest-growing entertainment categories in the world — a digitally native, globally connected audience that brands are racing to reach. Through a strategic partnership with a leading regional esports operator, Apex now brings the same rigour we apply to traditional sport — commercial structuring, event delivery, broadcast, and brand integration — into competitive gaming. Whether it's putting a brand's name on a tournament or building a new event from concept to final whistle, we operate end to end.

What We Offer

Capabilities

Tournament Sponsorship
We connect brands to the right competitive properties — packaging and placing sponsorship across tournaments, leagues, and teams to put your name in front of a young, engaged, hard-to-reach audience.
Event Creation
From a single marquee tournament to a full season of competition, we design and create esports events from the ground up — format, prize structure, venue, and the show that surrounds the play.
Tournament Operations
Online and on-stage execution — brackets, moderation, officiating, player and team management, and the operational backbone that keeps a competition running cleanly start to finish.
Broadcast & Production
Studio-grade live production, on-screen graphics, casting talent, and multi-platform streaming — built to broadcast standard and packaged for TV, digital, and social.
Brand Activation
In-game, on-stage, and around the event — we build activations that turn a sponsorship into genuine engagement with the gaming community, not just a logo on a banner.
Audience & Community
We grow and engage the audience that surrounds every competition — content, creators, and community programming that extend reach well beyond the event itself.
Across Every Arena

Esports Titles We Specialize In

From battle royales to fighting games and MOBAs — we operate across the titles that define competitive gaming today, building events and brand activations around the games audiences actually play.
